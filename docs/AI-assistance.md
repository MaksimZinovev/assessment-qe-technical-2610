# AI Assistance

## What was outsourced to AI

- Majority of implementation when my intent was captured, plan and design clarified.
- During planning I used specialized skills, prompting and hardness to document how we close the gaps in design, capture and document decisions.

## What I did not delegated to AI

- Planning. I outlined the high level plan, my intent, scope  and main design choices.  
- Troubleshooting issues. When AI agent got stuck or spent too much effort on issues. For example, I had to manually find better locators and asked agent to use role based locators instead of CSS.
- Steering AI agent.
  - I broke down entire process into milestones.
  - I asked main agent to act as orchestrator and work collaboratively with implementation agent to build and follow the plan.
  - I asked main agent to review the work done by implementation agent.
  - I selectively reviewed the work of each agent.  For example, executed tests, checked the code, asked for corrections (add test steps, "describe" blocks)
